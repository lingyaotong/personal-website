## Research Interests
I'm generally interested in how to improve access to and outcomes of psychotherapy for depression. I also have particular research interests in global mental health, personalised mental health treatments, and advanced statistics.

## Expertise
- I use R(mostly), Stata(increasingly), and SPSS(occasionally)
- I'm experienced with systematic review and meta-analysis from randomised-controlled trials (pairwise, network, and individual participant data meta-analysis)
- I'm developing skills using causal inference methods for observational records (G-computation, Target Maximum Likelihood Estimation, and Causal forest)
- I'm also gaining expertise in developing and validating clinical prediction modelling (prognostic, decision-support tool)
